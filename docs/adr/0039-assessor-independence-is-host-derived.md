# Assessor independence is host-derived

Independence is validated from a host-derived Assessor Identity binding Role Run, distinct Agent instance, Governed Provider, model deployment, and execution lineage; a role label or persona assertion is insufficient. Distinct Independence Groups use separate Role Runs and Agent instances by default, and no Developer identity may assess its own implementation. Effective Policy may require additional Provider or model diversity for high-risk groups, but v0.2 does not impose different vendors as a universal product prerequisite.
