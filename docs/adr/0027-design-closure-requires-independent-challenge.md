# Design Closure requires independent challenge

Planner cannot close the Design Ledger merely by returning no questions. Kernel recognizes Design Closure only after the current Frontier is empty and an independent `design-completeness` Reviewer assessment passes; when the Ledger contained User Decisions, a final `design-closure` decision depending on all of them must also be confirmed by the user. A truly zero-decision Mission may close after independent review alone, balancing low-friction routine work with explicit shared understanding for consequential design.
